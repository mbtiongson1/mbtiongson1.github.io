SIGNALWhat deserves attention right now?
A board to read, not a wall of numbers. The bigger the line, the more urgent it is. Unled is the plain fact: an active group with no Leader or Assistant Leader on record in Rock. Under strain counts the Critical and Thin groups; each one says exactly what is wrong, never a blended score. Watch is softer pressure, the cheapest place to prevent next month's strain. The fourth line is Retention, and it says Unavailable: we don't track how many people stay in a group over time, so this page shows the gap rather than a number nobody can stand behind. The strip below the board is the whole field, one tick per group, sorted Critical → Healthy; the groups you have filtered to stay lit and the rest dim. Arrow keys walk the strip, Enter opens a group. Clicking a board line reshapes the whole page.

PLOTBOARDWhere should I look first, and why?
A locality operations board, not a map application. Every mark is a locality and nothing else: where it sits is the locality's own coarse centroid, how big it is and the numeral inside it are both the number of Connect Groups there, and the label is the place name. Only the emphasis changes when you change the reading, so the same object answers 4 questions without becoming 4 objects. The board lights exactly the places the rail beside it ranks: a filled mark is one of the 8 the rail is naming, a ringed mark has the same signal lower down the list, and a quiet mark has nothing to say in this reading. Everything stays on the board either way; what changes is which places are speaking. Pressure ranks by how many groups are Thin or Critical, and the hue of a lit mark is the worst state actually present in it. One Critical group makes the place Critical, because that is the group somebody has to go and see. Capacity ranks and shades by open seats we can actually count; a place where nobody has set a capacity in Rock draws as a dashed outline and reads Unknown, which isn't a zero, and is never ranked as if it were. Network is presence, not a judgement, so it lights nothing: the size of a mark is the whole message. Opportunity says Unavailable: we can't yet count who asked to join against how many places there are, and a made-up number would be worse than an empty panel. The composition (which states, how many leaders, how much capacity) is in the rail beside the board, where there is room to name it. Picking a campus moves the board and every other station together; Recenter only moves the camera. Zoom stops at locality because the data stops at locality: no street, no landmark and no person's coordinate is on this page or in this build. PRESSUREIs leadership overloaded?
Each mark is a group: left to right is people per leader, the size of the mark is how many active members it has, and the colour is the group's state. This station was designed with a second axis (how many people stay) because a full group people stay in is a different pastoral conversation from a well-supported group people drift away from. We don't track that yet, so the marks sit on one rail: how high or low a mark sits means nothing here, and the page says so rather than spreading the dots out to look like data. Unled groups sit in their own labelled column instead of being plotted as if one leader were carrying everybody. The line at one leader per 12 people is a working guide, said out loud rather than hidden. The table under the chart is the same groups, reachable by keyboard, with no picture required.

COHORTSDo some kinds of group struggle more than others?
Who a group is for, against what kind of group it is, both straight from Rock. A group can be more than one kind, so one group can sit in more than one column and the columns deliberately don't add up to the row. The number is groups in your current selection; the bar and the wash are the share currently Thin or Critical. A hatched cell is a shape the church doesn't have anywhere. That's different from a zero. A cell holding 0 is a real zero: the shape exists, and no group in this selection has it. It describes, it never explains: a hot cell says "these groups currently differ from the rest," never "being this kind of group causes it." Click or press Enter on a cell and the whole page follows it.

RHYTHMWhen does the Connect network actually breathe?
The day and the time of day groups meet, then the week-by-week attendance table underneath. The times of day are this page's reading, not a Rock field: Rock stores the meet-up time as free text, so a time we can't read as a clock time lands in Not set rather than being guessed into an evening the group may not meet in. In the table below, no meeting on record is its own state: Rock holds no meeting for that group that week at all, which is neither a cancelled meeting nor one nobody entered attendance for. It never counts as a group going quiet. Click a pocket to focus the whole page on it; what you see here is worth exploring, not a verdict.

MODESDoes the room change the pattern?
Every kind of meeting place in the field, compared on the same scales: the same mix of group states, the same share of meetings answered, the same seat arithmetic. How many people stay isn't something we track yet, and every panel says so on purpose; an empty row would read as agreement. A mode with no groups in your selection reports a real 0: we looked, and there are none. Click a mode to focus the whole page on it.

QUEUEWhich exact groups should I open next?
Only after you have read the field: the 8 groups under the most pressure in your current selection, never a spreadsheet. Unled groups come first, then the ones whose attendance has gone quiet, then the ones where each leader is carrying the most people, then the ones with no capacity set in Rock. The order uses only what this page can actually work out: something we don't have never quietly counts as healthy, and never counts at all. Every entry says why now in plain terms: no hidden score, no person's name, no group name. The 6 squares are the last 6 weeks; a week with no meeting on record in Rock is drawn as its own state and never counted as a group going quiet. "Open Connect" lands on the group's attendance page at connect.example.invalid.

CANOPYWhich parts of the organization carry the field?
The canopy is weight, not an org chart: how wide a band is, is how many groups it carries, and the fill is the mix of their states, bottom to top. The heaviest bands come first. A band is named after the highest level of the Connect structure whose name is a place in the organisation. The Region and Cluster levels in between are named after the people who lead them, so this page shows only the level word and a reference number, never the parent's name. No person's name appears anywhere on this page. The structure is uneven: a group that sits directly under a band is counted where it actually sits, and the band says how many. Click a band to focus the page on it.

How a group's state is decided

A group's state is the worst of the things we can actually check: Leadership, Meetings and attendance, and Capacity, never a blended score. A group with no Leader or Assistant Leader on record is Critical whatever else is true. Every group this page surfaces says plainly which of those is wrong.

Two more things belong in that picture and aren't in it: Retention (how many people stay) and Progression between groups. We don't track either yet. They are not quietly counted as healthy and they aren't dropped either: a group is judged on leadership, capacity, and attendance alone, and it says out loud which parts we couldn't check.

States that aren't the same thing

  • Attendance entered: the meeting is on record and someone entered who came.
  • Meeting cancelled: the group told us they did not meet. That is an answer, not a gap.
  • Attendance not entered: the meeting is on record and nobody wrote down who came. This is the one that means silence.
  • No meeting on record: Rock holds no meeting for that group that week at all. Not a cancelled meeting, and not a missed one.
  • Unavailable: we couldn't load this from Rock. Hollow and dashed everywhere it appears, and it always says why.
  • 0 A real zero: we looked, and the answer is none. It is written as the numeral, in reading ink, and never dashed or hollow.
  • ? Still loading: shimmering and purple, carrying a question mark. The page is still working; this isn't a value.

Boundaries this page keeps

Rock stays the record; this page reads it and never changes it. connect.example.invalid is where the work happens; every group link here lands on that group's attendance page and nowhere else. Directory is still the place for questions about a person; it isn't where a group link should take you.
