# Field Atlas

- **Scope:** `/worlds/field-atlas/`; Experience mode.
- **Audience / job:** A design-focused visitor arriving from the portfolio hub; scan named work, inspect a real interface, and follow a verified project or profile link without learning map gestures.
- **Proof:** Reviewed static captures from the owner's Rock Dashboards fictional-data prototype and watershed WIP fictional-data visualization. Image disclosures remain adjacent to each artifact. No geographic inference, client result, or live-system claim.
- **Memorable moment:** A large real UI capture sits on a chart-like survey sheet; choosing a name in the index brings its image and plain-language context to the foreground. The map is a browsing metaphor, not project geography.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** An atlas of interface work where the artifact is the surveyed territory, not a backdrop to a conventional portfolio hero. Refuse a row of equal cards and faux geographic coordinates.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep blue-green chart field, fine contour strokes, bone-white typographic ink, cyan river trace, and restrained lime survey pins. Crisp ruled frames and annotation tabs carry navigation. A display face with expedition-poster heft contrasts with compact legible body text; neither a decorative map nor a generic dashboard skin.

**STORY:** A visitor sees the Rock prototype capture immediately, reads its fictional-data boundary, switches via a named project index or scrolls to a second artifact, then can visit the shared worlds and public contact links. The index works without JavaScript.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow masthead spans the top. At desktop, a large left title and named index occupy roughly a third of the width; an oversized, legible Rock capture owns the right, with a compact disclosure directly beneath. A watershed image shapes the lower chart field but does not obscure the UI. On mobile, the title and project index precede a full-width capture and its disclosure.

**FORM:** Field Atlas, assigned direction from seed `5b4faa74`; code-led translation of the approved decision comp as a concept reference, not shipping imagery. Signature interaction: the named index jumps to full project sections while the chart's route trace responds subtly to the active project; motion is optional and reduced-motion safe.
