## Direction contract

**THESIS:** An atlas of interface work where the artifact is the surveyed territory, not a backdrop to a conventional portfolio hero. Refuse a row of equal cards and faux geographic coordinates.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep blue-green chart field, fine contour strokes, bone-white typographic ink, cyan river trace, and restrained lime survey pins. Crisp ruled frames and annotation tabs carry navigation. A display face with expedition-poster heft contrasts with compact legible body text; neither a decorative map nor a generic dashboard skin.

**STORY:** A visitor sees the compiled People prototype immediately, reads its fictional-data boundary, tries its interactive chart controls or opens it full size, then follows a named project index to the watershed study and the shared worlds. The index works without JavaScript.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow masthead spans the top. At desktop, a large left title and named index occupy roughly a third of the width; an oversized frame for the real interactive compiled People page owns the right, with a visible fictional-data disclosure and a full-page launch beside it. The watershed image enters the lower chart field without obscuring the UI. On mobile, the index precedes the framed artifact and its disclosure.
