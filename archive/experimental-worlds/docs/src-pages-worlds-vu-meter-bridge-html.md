# VU Meter Bridge — surface brief

## Scope and visitor mode
Complete, responsive portfolio experience at `/worlds/vu-meter-bridge/`. Mode: **Experience**. This route is a distinct way to inspect the shared six-project archive, not a mock dashboard or a shared-site skin.

## Visitor and task
Design-focused hiring managers, collaborators, and peers arrive from a profile or GitHub. They choose a named project channel, inspect its real safe artifact or external link, understand its publication status, and continue to another channel or portfolio world. The evidence is exclusively `data/projects.json` plus the artifacts and links it names. Keep every project title, disclosure, caption, and context adjacent to its artifact; Gaia Research remains external-link-only. No invented metrics, values, sound, live connections, claims, or preview artwork.

## Direction
The owner-pinned, approved VU-meter bridge comp is the first-viewport contract. Treat the recording desk as a precise physical wayfinding instrument: dark steel frame, measured walnut rails, pale instrument faces, black needles, and one red calibration zone. Named projects are the channels, not dashboard KPIs. Actual catalog evidence, not the generated comp, fills the monitor. Avoid decorative knobs, faux measurements, sound effects, and a generic project-card grid.

## Memorable moment
The visitor selects a clearly named project channel; its black selection needle settles, and the actual preview or an honest no-local-preview link appears in the monitor bay. The needle communicates selection only.

## Unresolved decisions
Final responsive breakpoints and component dimensions follow implementation. The source catalog remains the sole source of project facts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A recording-desk bridge makes six real project channels the primary navigation and brings the selected artifact into one working monitor bay. It refuses the familiar thumbnail-card portfolio: selection, not a grid of equal previews, determines what is under inspection.

OWN-WORLD: A charcoal steel chassis and restrained walnut rails hold pale cream instrument faces; fine black tick marks and a single fixed red calibration zone give the controls their physical precision. Barlow Condensed carries the dial lettering; Barlow carries readable artifact context. Knobs are not decoration. The needle and a clear text state identify selection only; neither behaves like a meter reading.

STORY: Visitors read the maker and choose a named project channel. The selected project's authentic embedded demo, still image, or link-only state occupies the monitor. Its catalog disclosure stays beside the evidence; a direct action opens the original at full size or opens the verified public site. The six-world navigation remains available throughout.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At desktop, a slim maker/world header sits above a full-width bridge of six evenly measured named channel controls. Below, one dark monitor chassis fills most of the width; the selected channel's title, kind, disclosure, and summary sit in a narrow left information bay, while its actual artifact occupies the dominant right screen. Walnut is limited to structural edge rails, and the fixed red meter zone is small. On mobile, the channel chooser becomes one full-width channel at a time with an explicit selected label; the selected project's information and artifact reflow into one column without scaling the desktop grid.

FORM: `data/projects.json` is the sole source of project titles, summaries, disclosures, artifacts, and verified links. The committed Experience form is the owner-pinned analog VU meter bridge in `.impeccable/mocks/decision/challenger-vu.webp` (prompt sidecar beside it). No new decision-board round. Preserve the comp's channel/monitor topology and material character, but replace its unverified screen with catalog-backed evidence; never publish or trace its pixels.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
